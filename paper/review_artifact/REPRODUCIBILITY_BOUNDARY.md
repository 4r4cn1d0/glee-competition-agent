# Reproducibility boundary

## What this bundle reproduces

- Arithmetic over the released deidentified checkpoint.
- Aggregate counts used in the behavior analysis.
- The complete two-window final-policy census and archive-coverage arithmetic.
- Aggregate semantic-parser miss counts for the audited pre-close persuasion
  subcohort, without message content.
- The bounded experiment-registry flow.
- The payoff-to-rank counterexample and selected game-theoretic identities.
- Selected, disclosed policy behavior on synthetic states.
- Four figures and three rendered tables.

## What this bundle cannot establish

- It cannot reclassify language because raw messages are withheld.
- It cannot re-audit joins between actions and outcomes because source records
  and direct identifiers are withheld.
- It cannot rederive randomized assignment because assignment inputs are
  withheld.
- It cannot replay opponent responses or estimate unobserved counterfactuals.
- It cannot prove that the reference functions are equivalent to the withheld
  production implementation. They are executable specifications of selected
  disclosed branches only.
- It cannot independently verify service receipt, official placement, or the
  account-level competition result. Final ratings, public game count, and
  placements are released snapshot inputs whose internal consistency is
  checked here, not independently authenticated service responses.
- It cannot identify the standalone causal value of a component inside the
  adaptive submitted trajectory.

## Privacy and operational safety

The bundle contains no credentials, direct participant identifiers, opponent
labels, raw dialogue, wall-clock records, assignment salts, host information,
process information, or live controls. The reference policy has no network,
environment, logging, filesystem-mutation, or runtime-overlay interface.

The aggregate inputs preserve enough information to verify the paper's stated
arithmetic while deliberately preventing reconstruction of individual games.
This is an aggregate-first review artifact, not a public data release.
