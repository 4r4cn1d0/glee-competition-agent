                                                         Abstract
 1             Games in Language Based Economic Environments (GLEE) evaluates agents in
 2             multiround bargaining, bilateral negotiation, and repeated persuasion. It converts
 3             payoff to a same-configuration, same-role percentile before opponent adjustment
 4             and rating. We report our submitted agent’s full development as an adaptive
 5             system, not a frozen policy. It routed visible states among game-theoretic owners,
 6             empirical rank heuristics, history rules, and legal fallbacks. A census of 46,316 local
 7             assignments and 1,348 frozen rating polls traces closed-form solvers, randomized
 8             probes, failed imitation and stalling, rollbacks, and a deterministic final router.
 9             The agent finished second in the 2026 agent track and third combined, closing at
10             2590.02 after 46,564 official rated-game increments, with family ratings 2804.82,
11             2620.36, and 2344.87. A separate final-policy audit covers 245 assignments and
12             2,288 returned actions; mean displayed changes per game were +1.26, −0.05,
13             and +0.76 across the three families. Agreement and payoff alone were unsafe
14             targets. The evidence supports a competitive, traceable adaptive system, but gives
15             no component causal credit for placement.


16   1    Objective, inspiration, and contribution
17   GLEE spans 960 configurations that vary role, horizon, information, communication, and economic
18   parameters [GLEE Competition Organizing Team, 2026]. Bargaining divides a pot, negotiation trades
19   under public or private values, and persuasion repeatedly recommends products whose quality a buyer
20   learns only after purchase. Shapira et al. study how GLEE’s agent and market design affect self-gain,
21   efficiency, and fairness [Shapira et al., 2024]. We instead ask how one competition agent should be
22   designed and revised when success depends on relative outcomes within each configuration and role.
23   For payoff Y in configuration c, role r, and reference pool at time τ , let q = Qc,r,τ (Y ) be its raw
24   empirical percentile and qe the undocumented opponent-adjusted value. A normally completed game
25   updates the unshrunk family rating as

41   2     Agent design and strategy evolution

42   The physical submission slot changed policies throughout the competition. We therefore call it the
43   focal agent and reserve final policy for the bounded configuration first observed on August 29. On
44   each final-policy turn, a deterministic dispatcher mapped visible state s = (g, r, T, I, C, θi , x, h),
45   denoting family, role, horizon, information, communication, visible role-private type, public economic
46   parameters, and history, to an ordered family policy. The first eligible owner returned a candidate
47   action, numeric postguards and schema coercion produced the wire action, and an exception invoked
48   a legal fallback. The final windows ran with the LLM disabled. Earlier persuasion development did
49   use generated wording, which was later removed after it converted less often than templates.

102   2,288 final actions and gates_fired for 1,207. We can verify what action left the local policy and
103   appeared in replay, but not reconstruct a selected owner for every turn.

104   4   Results, failures, and lessons
105   The account led by the focal agent finished second in the agent track and third combined at 2590.02.
106   Figure 1 and Table 2 show that this endpoint was produced by adaptation across all families, not by a
107   monotone climb or a bargaining-only policy. Using first-own-action and half-open phases ending
108   August 22 10:54:50, August 24 20:05:52, and August 29 05:27:36 UTC, B/N/P assignments were
109   5,983/5,972/5,967 in rapid search, 6,322/6,335/6,321 in measurement, and 3,063/3,028/3,080 in
110   deterministic assembly. Each development phase exposed all three families substantially.
111   Opponent observation influenced the search but was never treated as proof. Three then-leading
112   negotiation profiles opened at roughly .92 to 1.05 of visible surplus while our opening was about
113   .681. This motivated a .95 open-claim challenger and later observed-leader buyer paths. Field drift
114   and agreement-conditioned samples made direct imitation unsafe, so the broad clone was assigned by
115   game hash and judged on all assignments. The observed correlation explains why the strategy was
116   tested; the stopped experiment explains why it was not promoted wholesale.
117   Negative results changed the policy. Bargaining stonewall and economic stall were removed after
118   live divergence and exploit tests. A promising high-share delay rule was found unreachable; under
119   the later stack, waiting beat immediate acceptance in all 14 joined triggers by 21.77 pot points on
120   average. In negotiation, a randomized 91-game leader clone had a positive point estimate but an
121   interval crossing zero, 6.14 percentage points more no-deals, and a negative focal-agent stratum,
122   so it was stopped. A later score-floor challenger closed only 7 of 27 resolved fires and was rolled
123   back. In persuasion, LLM-generated wording converted at 46.3% versus 55.3% for templates, and a
124   randomized backload policy lost by .391 raw-percentile points in its strict profitable cells. Both were
125   removed.
126   Failures remained at close. In one buyer game, four warnings escaped the final persuasion veto; the
127   agent bought all four products and each was LOW. After three passed positive recommendations,
128   only 8 of 664 observed targeted LOW seller recommendations converted. The final credibility reset
129   was copied before its source experiment reached its precommitted efficacy read. Finally, the clearest
130   same-overlay seesaw retained every family: across 79 August 27 games, bargaining gained 21.03
131   rating points while negotiation lost 33.78 and persuasion lost 29.42. Overall fell from about 2592.11
132   to 2578.05. These are descriptive diagnostics in a nonstationary field, not component effects.

133   Reproducibility and conclusion. Frozen receipts preserve source, overlay, aggregates, rating
134   snapshots, final replays, and mathematical checks. We plan to release sanitized production-equivalent
135   source and verifiers; private replays await data-use approval.
136   The second-place result supports the competitiveness of measured revision across all three families.
137   Game theory supplied local owners, rank awareness changed acceptable risks, and action auditing
138   separated intended from returned behavior. No single owner has causal credit for the placement.
