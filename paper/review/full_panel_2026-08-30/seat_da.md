## Strongest Counter-Argument

Even granting the manuscript’s unusually careful disclaimers, its core empirical story remains underidentified. The headline ratings summarize 46,383 games from an adaptive lifetime trajectory, whereas the frozen final policy contributed only 19 bargaining, 19 negotiation, and 25 persuasion games—just 3.73%, 3.73%, and 4.88% of endpoint raw-rating weight. No prospective comparison identifies the final policy’s value. Indeed, none of the 35 named development records passed a precommitted efficacy-promotion rule; the registry omits handoff-only candidates and threshold sweeps, while six components were live or retained without promotion. The resulting “theory plus negative evidence narrowed the bank” story is therefore coherent retrospectively but not validated prospectively.

Nor does the behavior analysis close this gap. Its most memorable evidence combines 25 games pooled across three profiles, simulations, selected reached states, and historical warning cases that the appendix concedes do not establish that banked V1 changed those actions. A complete 63-game process census exists, but not a complete commit-to-owner-to-server manifest. The planned artifact arrives only after acceptance and will still withhold the game IDs and joins needed to re-derive assignment and receipt.

After removing provisional leaderboard status and elementary rank, bargaining, trade, and Bayesian identities, what remains is a disciplined single-agent forensic case study and an action-audit checklist. That may be useful, but the paper neither establishes this audit contract as novel against adjacent systems practice nor demonstrates transfer beyond one highly adapted competition stack. Most decisively, the submission itself declares unresolved possible-human-opponent ethics and says it must not be submitted yet. As written, acceptance is blocked; an award is substantially less defensible.

## Ignored Alternative Explanations/Paths

- Endpoint rank may reflect early policies, favorable configuration and opponent mixtures, activity/decay mechanics, field drift, and best-profile selection rather than the final bank.
- The complete-versus-hidden negotiation closure contrast may reflect feasibility support, horizons, roles, opponents, and era composition—not information visibility itself.
- Cross-profile behavioral consistency may arise from shared code ancestry or selected reachable states rather than robust policy ownership.
- A stronger scientific path is a prospective frozen-policy experiment or a multi-agent validation of the audit contract, rather than reconstructing effectiveness from the winning trajectory.
- Warning-associated LOW outcomes could establish a parser coverage counterexample without establishing that the deployed veto caused, prevented, or even encountered those purchases.
