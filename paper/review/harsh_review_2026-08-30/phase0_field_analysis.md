# Phase 0 Field Analysis and Reviewer Configuration

## Review target

- Manuscript: `Rank-Aware Routing and Returned-Action Audits in GLEE`
- Target: NeurIPS 2026 IAB Workshop Competition Paper Track
- Article type: four-page competition paper with references, mandatory checklist, and a long technical appendix
- Research form: adaptive-agent case study combining a live competition endpoint, bounded observational action censuses, randomized and nonrandomized experiments, formal policy descriptions, and a reproducibility artifact
- Maturity: late working draft, not submission-ready while author metadata and the human-opponent ethics determination remain unresolved

## Review scope and handling

The author explicitly requested this local review. Manuscript content remains local and is not submitted to external services. Public venue materials and public references may be checked externally without including unpublished manuscript text. This is a working review for the author, not an official editorial decision or award prediction.

## Role-separated panel

1. Journal-Fit Reviewer: NeurIPS workshop competition-paper editor focused on contribution, four-page prioritization, and track compliance.
2. Peer Reviewer 1, Methodology: adaptive online experimentation and agent-evaluation methodologist focused on cohorts, denominators, selection bias, missingness, causal scope, and reproducibility.
3. Peer Reviewer 2, Domain: bargaining and information-economics researcher focused on equations, equilibrium terminology, percentile objectives, strategy fidelity, and literature support.
4. Peer Reviewer 3, Perspective: agent-behavior, reproducibility, ethics, and scientific-visualization reviewer focused on the required behavior analysis, artifact boundary, privacy, and whether each figure earns scarce space.
5. Devil's Advocate: fixed adversarial seat focused on the strongest rejection case, post hoc storytelling, cherry-picking, alternative explanations, and the paper's actual scientific delta.

The roles are separated by task and context. They are not claimed to be independent error processes.
