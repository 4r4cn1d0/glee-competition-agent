# Panel Adjudication and Disagreement Log

## Decision rule

The synthesis did not average reviewer scores. Each critical and major objection was checked against the current source, exact PDF, frozen data, and available artifact. An objection can be:

- validated;
- partly validated because the manuscript discloses the limitation but the evidence gap remains;
- rejected because the current manuscript or evidence answers it;
- an external precondition that cannot be resolved by manuscript wording.

## Genuine disagreements

### Should the paper lead with the audit method or the competition-agent case study?

The venue-fit reviewer sees the most credible award case in the combination of strategy, rank objective, and disciplined negative evidence. The Devil's Advocate argues that the audit lacks comparative validation and therefore cannot carry originality by itself.

**Arbitration:** lead with the runner-up adaptive-agent case study. Present the returned-action audit as the evidence discipline that makes the case credible. Promote the audit to a general method only if conformance and comparative validation are added.

### Is the rating trajectory worth main-paper space?

The visual reviewer recommends moving it into the core because it directly shows the adaptive endpoint and rating reversals. The Devil's Advocate prefers conformance and proxy-validation figures because trajectory cannot identify effects.

**Arbitration:** use the trajectory only after the conformance or audit-coverage visual earns its place. It should replace the one-row result table and repeated chronology, carry an explicitly descriptive caption, and not be presented as efficacy evidence.

### Should the paper add a strategy diagram?

One venue-oriented suggestion favored a compact routing diagram to explain the agent. The visual, methodology, and theory reviews rejected a decorative architecture diagram because it would imply an evidence chain that is not established.

**Arbitration:** do not add an architecture figure. Use a compact strategy table with exact triggers, actions, postguards, fallbacks, and evidence labels. Tables are better suited to precise policy specification; the limited figure budget should go to empirical evidence.

### Does the current paper merit Reject or Major Revision?

The Devil's Advocate says Reject as submitted and Strong Reject for Best Paper. Other seats say Major Revision with plausible acceptance.

**Arbitration:** both are compatible once the decision stage is separated. The exact file should not be submitted because external and metadata blockers remain. As a scientific project, it merits Major Revision rather than abandonment because the mathematics, endpoint, and negative evidence are real. For Best Paper, the current version is below the bar until conformance, policy completeness, and independent novelty evidence improve.

## Rejected objections

The panel does not support the following harsher claims:

- The scoring mathematics is fundamentally wrong.
- The hidden-seller objective is presented as a visible-cell optimum.
- The persuasion obedience threshold is used as a theorem for repeated cheap talk.
- The clone is presented as a successful causal experiment.
- Rating chronology is presented as identifying field drift.
- The persuasion parser miss is presented as a population prevalence estimate.
- The second-place endpoint is explicitly attributed to one component.
- The aggregate artifact visibly leaks raw messages or opponent identities.

The manuscript already limits these claims appropriately. Revision should preserve those boundaries.
