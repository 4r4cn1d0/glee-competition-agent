# Ordered Revision Roadmap

## Revision principle

Do not add more theory or more pages until the evidence chain and narrative hierarchy are fixed. The paper already contains enough material. The revision should remove duplicated caveats, make the runner-up policy reconstructible, and spend the recovered space on direct behavior evidence.

## Phase 0: clear submission preconditions

These tasks precede scientific polishing because the current file cannot safely be submitted without them.

1. Replace all author placeholders with the actual single-blind author information.
2. Obtain the applicable organizer and institutional ethics/data-use determination for possible human-opponent interactions.
3. Resolve permission and licensing for any aggregate data or sanitized source that will be shared.
4. Confirm the allowed artifact channel and whether any technical appendix or supplement is permitted.
5. Make the Overleaf default root compile only the exact submission-safe PDF.
6. Verify OpenReview profiles, conflicts, author order, and the required public agent identifier.
7. Archive a durable official final-board source.

**Exit criterion:** the paper, checklist, artifact statement, and release plan all describe the same documented permissions and exact submission package.

## Phase 2: rebuild the Agent Behavior Analysis

### Preferred analysis

For all 2,288 focal actions, or at minimum the complete 63-game close window, derive a turn-level table with:

- family and role;
- visible state needed by the policy;
- eligible owner set;
- selected owner;
- independently specified action;
- pre-coercion plan;
- postguarded and coerced returned action;
- exact match indicator;
- override or fallback reason;
- local error or unresolved status;
- joined outcome status.

Aggregate by family and major owner. Print eligible, selected, exact match, override, fallback, unresolved, and joined denominators.

### If full reconstruction is impossible

1. Relabel the 245-game object as a returned-behavior census.
2. Describe the 25-game bargaining audit, 800-game negotiation simulation, and four-game persuasion audit as separate auxiliary cohorts.
3. Remove any statement that implies the focal final policy was shown to conform end to end.
4. Change `audit` to `evidence` or `partial audit` in the title and abstract.

Report the complete flow:

- 46,564 public rated games;
- 46,316 local records;
- 46,301 canonical terminal joins;

- 15 additional unresolved finals;

**Exit criterion:** the required behavior section directly says what matched design, how it was checked, and what remains unknown, with raw denominators.

## Recommended decision gate

After Phase 2, make one explicit decision:

### Gate A: complete conformance is recoverable

Keep the returned-action audit as a central contribution. Use the conformance figure, release the strongest privacy-safe policy artifact, and make the award case around a competitive agent plus end-to-end execution evidence.

### Gate B: complete conformance is not recoverable

Narrow the paper to a rigorous adaptive-agent case study. Retitle it, replace `audit establishes` with `bounded evidence shows`, and emphasize strategy, failure analysis, and lessons. This can still be a strong competition paper, but it should not claim a validated audit method.

Do not postpone this decision until the final copy-edit. It determines the title, abstract, figures, contribution claims, and artifact promise.
